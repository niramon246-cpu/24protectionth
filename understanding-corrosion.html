Insight #1

Freshwater Does Not Mean Low Risk

Freshwater is often perceived as less corrosive than seawater. However, some of the most damaging failures occur where deterioration remains hidden until it is too late.

Localized corrosion, microbiological activity and under-deposit corrosion can develop long before visible signs appear.

The greatest risks are not always the most visible ones.
